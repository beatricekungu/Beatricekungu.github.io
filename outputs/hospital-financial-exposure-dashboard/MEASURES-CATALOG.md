# Measures Catalog

This project uses a dedicated Measures table to keep the dashboard's KPI logic centralized and easier to maintain.

## Measure Categories

### Admissions and Utilization

- Total Admissions
- Total Admissions Display
- Total Encounters
- Repeat Encounters
- Average Encounters per Patient
- Total Visits Label

### Readmissions

- Readmissions
- Readmitted Patients
- Readmission Rate
- Admissions Trend Label

### Cost and Financial Exposure

- Average Cost Card
- Average Cost Change Label
- Average Cost per Visit
- Total Claim Cost

### Length of Stay

- Average Length of Stay Hours
- Average Stay Card
- Length of Stay Trend Label

### Insurance and Coverage

- Claim Coverage Rate
- Coverage Rate Label
- Coverage Status
- Insured Procedures
- Insured Procedures Card
- Procedure Coverage Display
- Procedure Coverage Rate
- Payer Coverage Share Label
- Total Payer Coverage
- Total Procedures

### Report Operations

- KPI Comparison Year
- Last Refresh Display

## Why This Matters

Centralizing measures makes the report easier to audit, explain, and update. Instead of placing business logic directly inside visuals, the dashboard relies on a clear measure layer for repeatable KPI definitions.
