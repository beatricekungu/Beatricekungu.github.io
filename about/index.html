About

GRC and cloud security consultant with 7+ years connecting control requirements, technical evidence, and business decisions across enterprise and federal client programs.

GRC · Cyber Risk · Cloud Security · IAM

I make security requirements usable—for the teams implementing controls and the leaders accountable for risk.

My experience spans risk and compliance, cloud data protection, identity governance, privacy-sensitive incident response, Power BI reporting, and database operations. I turn complex requirements into clear findings, accountable remediation plans, validated controls, and decision-ready reporting.

The case studies in this portfolio show how I work: define the risk, build or assess the control, preserve the evidence, explain the limitation, and connect the result to a business decision. I am targeting GRC, cyber risk, cloud security, and IAM roles where cross-functional ownership matters.
