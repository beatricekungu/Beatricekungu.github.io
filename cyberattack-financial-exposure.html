Challenge context

What Maven asked analysts to answer

The Maven Hospital Challenge asks participants to act as an analytics consultant for Massachusetts General Hospital and build a high-level KPI report for executives. I am using that prompt to showcase Power BI storytelling, healthcare operations analysis, financial exposure framing, and governance-minded reporting.

Question 01

How many patients have been admitted or readmitted over time?

Executive answer: The overview shows 974 distinct patients, 854 repeat patients, and an 87.7% repeat visit rate, with a yearly patient-volume and readmission trend.

Question 02

How long are patients staying in the hospital, on average?

Executive answer: The report includes an Average Length of Stay Hours measure so leaders can monitor capacity pressure and compare stay patterns by patient or encounter group.

Question 03

How much is the average cost per visit?

Executive answer: Average Cost per Visit and Total Claim Cost measures frame visit-level financial exposure across encounter, payer, and procedure views.

Question 04

How many procedures are covered by insurance?

Executive answer: Insured Procedures, Procedure Coverage Rate, and Coverage Status logic identify covered versus uncovered procedures and reimbursement risk.
